# MergerScan

Proof-of-concept AI-powered merger and acquisition (M&A) news intelligence platform built with Python and Streamlit.

## Overview

MergerScan automates the process of scanning press releases and news articles for potential merger and acquisition activity. The platform scrapes publicly available news sources, applies LLM-based classification to identify M&A-related content, and performs additional AI-assisted web research on the entities involved.

The application is deployed on Streamlit Community Cloud and provides an interactive dashboard for analysts to review, filter, and research merger-related developments.

## Key Features

- Automated scraping of press releases and news articles
- LLM-based classification of merger and acquisition related news
- Storage of filtered articles and research results in SQLite
- AI-assisted web research using Perplexity searches
- Interactive Streamlit dashboard for article review and filtering
- Research drill-down view with supporting URLs and citations
- Built-in AI chat assistant for additional queries and analysis
